# Globin Console

Combine two *HBB* alleles into a working diagnosis of a haemoglobinopathy (interface in French).

**Open it:** https://clairevaltin.github.io/GlobinConsole/

## What it does

Globin Console helps interpret β-globin results: β-thalassaemias, sickle cell disease and haemoglobin variants.

- **Genotype mode** – enter the variants found by sequencing (one or two alleles) and get the expected phenotype straight away
- **Electrophoresis mode** – enter measured fractions (HbA, HbA2, HbF, HbS, HbC, HbE, and others) to get a diagnostic orientation
- **Context** – age, MCV, MCH, ferritin and recent transfusion refine the interpretation
- **Expected electrophoresis profile** simulated for the chosen genotype
- **Live database checks** against ClinVar and Ensembl VEP / gnomAD
- **Catalogue** of HBB variants, including Moroccan allele frequencies from the literature

HGVS notation uses the MANE Select transcript NM_000518.5 (GRCh38). Large deletions, Hb Lepore and deletional HPFH cannot be detected by Sanger sequencing. Always confirm with the full haematology workup (blood count, HPLC / electrophoresis).

Open the link above. Nothing to install.
