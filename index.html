Beta-globin variant interpretation

Combine two HBB alleles into a working diagnosis.

A curated reference of clinically significant HBB (β-globin) variants and a genotype combiner that reasons through zygosity and compound heterozygosity — β-thalassemia, sickle cell disease, HbC/E/D/O, Hb Lepore, and the benign variants that shouldn't be over-called.

⚠ Decision-support draft, not a final report. Genotype–phenotype correlation in hemoglobinopathies is variable and modified by co-inherited alpha-globin status, HBG promoter/HPFH variants, and other genetic modifiers. Large deletions, Hb Lepore, and HPFH deletions are not detectable by HBB-only Sanger sequencing — a clean point-mutation result doesn't rule them out. Confirm every call against current ClinVar/HbVar records and correlate with hematology (CBC, HPLC/electrophoresis) before it goes in a patient report.

How this was built

Methodology & sources

Coordinate system
All HGVS coding (c.) notation is relative to the MANE Select transcript NM_000518.5 on GRCh38.p14 (NC_000011.10), consistent with the HBB_MANE_sequences.docx reference in this project.
Primary sources
ClinVar (NCBI), HbVar (globin.bx.psu.edu), OMIM (141900, 603903, 613985), Agouti et al. 2008 and Belmokhtar et al. 2022 for Moroccan allele frequencies, and standard hemoglobinopathy literature.
Confidence marking
Confirmed — HGVS cross-checked directly against a live ClinVar record. Reported — literature/HbVar consensus, recommend re-confirming locally before it enters a patient-facing report.
Scope
~58 clinically significant variants were curated, not an exhaustive database (HbVar alone lists 900+ HBB alleles, most ultra-rare/uncharacterized). HPFH and large-deletion entries are included as interpretive notes, not Sanger-callable findings.
Globin Console — internal reference tool for HBB Sanger sequencing analysis. Built 2026-09-01. Not a diagnostic device. Every combined interpretation should be corroborated against current ClinVar/HbVar entries and full hematologic workup before clinical use.
